Bringing together five artists, Object Impermanence explores the shifting nature of identity, memory, relationships, and belief. Through figuration, material, symbolism, and gestures that preserve the human touch, the exhibition gives physical form to experiences that are constantly evolving yet leave lasting impressions.

Featured artist Melissa Capasso examines the fluid roles women inhabit throughout life, drawing on religious iconography, familiar symbols, and the relationship between seeing and believing. Her work questions how inherited belief systems transform through personal experience, exploring the space between tradition and individual identity. Together, the works offer an intimate meditation on impermanence, womanhood, spirituality, and the many ways we carry the past into who we become.
