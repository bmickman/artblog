October is officially underway, and downtown New York is easing into fall with a busy week of art, food, culture, and neighborhood happenings. From Tribeca and SoHo to the Lower East Side and East Village, there are new reasons to wander, linger, and make a plan somewhere below Canal Street.

And there is plenty more to explore beyond the galleries. This week’s picks take you through some of downtown’s most active neighborhoods, with opportunities to discover new art, sample something unexpected, browse local events, and settle into the particular energy of New York in early October. Consider this your guide to what’s happening around Tribeca, the LES, East Village, and SoHo this week.

One of this week’s art highlights is Cécile Roberfroid at Collective Z Gallery**,** where abstraction and the human figure come together in layered paintings and works on paper. Her vivid canvases shift between color and form, while figures surface through scraped paint, maps, torn paper, stitching, and other accumulated materials. The exhibition has a feeling of movement and discovery, rewarding the kind of close looking that makes a gallery visit worth the detour.
