This week’s featured artist is Hiroko Ohno, whose solo exhibition, Event Horizon, at Amos Eno Gallery explores the intersection of contemporary art, astronomy, geology, and our understanding of the universe. Featuring monumental paintings and sculptural elements, including works being presented publicly for the first time, the exhibition invites visitors to consider the invisible forces, vast timescales, and material histories that connect the cosmos to life on Earth. Drawing inspiration from the Event Horizon Telescope’s groundbreaking black hole imagery, Ohno transforms scientific questions into a compelling visual experience, using traditional Japanese mineral pigments and layered surfaces to evoke the relationship between what we see and what remains hidden.
